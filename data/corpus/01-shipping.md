# Northwind Gear — Shipping

Ground delivery reaches most addresses in 4 to 6 business days. Priority delivery arrives in 2 business days and costs $9.
Orders above $75 ship by ground at no charge. Orders placed before 2 pm Central time leave the warehouse the same day.
We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. Canadian parcels may take an extra 3 business days to clear customs.
