# Northwind Gear — Returns and Exchanges

Unused gear can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from the Orders page to print a prepaid label.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the parcel is scanned at our warehouse.
Worn footwear and opened fuel canisters cannot be returned for safety reasons. Exchanges for a different size ship free of charge.
